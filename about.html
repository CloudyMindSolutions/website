Jyrki Heinonen – Azure & Cloud Architect

Senior cloud architecture expertise, inclusive engineering leadership, and business-driven technology design.

Experience & Background

I have worked extensively within the Microsoft ecosystem for many years, using a broad range of its services. In recent years, I have focused on Azure and its native services.

In my roles, I have been guiding and building new cloud environments in line with company cloud strategies. Operating global SaaS services, scale and a high level of automation have been essential to ensure services can scale and be maintained reliably without continuously expanding headcount.

My scope of responsibility has spanned cloud security, identity and access management (IAM), automation, and cost management (FinOps), as well as building cloud governance models and reference architectures – in practice, a broad end-to-end scope. I have also supported sales with technical presales details and worked directly with enterprise customers throughout my career.

Leadership & Working Style

"In teams, I naturally take on an inclusive lead role and overall ownership of moving things forward.
