FinOps

Cost Management & FinOps Optimization

Fixed Analysis or Continuous

The Problem: "Azure bills are getting out of control, and cost reports are too complex to understand. We cannot tell which team or feature is driving expenses."

What we do:

  • Immediate waste reduction: orphaned resources, idle services, and suboptimal tiers
  • Tagging taxonomy and automated cost attribution to cost centers
  • Reserved Instances and Azure Savings Plans commitment modeling

Outcomes:

  • Accountability and ownership for resource consumption

Governance & Security

Cloud Governance, IAM & Security

Consulting / Hourly

The Problem: "Too many users hold permanent Owner or Contributor privileges, stale guest accounts clutter our tenant, and Azure Policy is either unconfigured or ignored."

What we do:

  • Enforce compliance policies via custom Azure Policy initiatives
  • Implement Least Privilege access and Privileged Identity Management (PIM)
  • Clean up Entra ID application registrations, secrets, and guest lifecycles
  • Adopt Managed Identities and Azure Key Vault to remove credentials from code

Outcomes:

  • Readiness for compliance frameworks and security audits (ISO 27001, NIS2, SOC 2)
  • Peace of mind knowing high-privilege access is temporary and audited
  • Automated guardrails preventing accidental security oversights
